Release notes

Choose your voice

2 November 2026 · Release: the next published release after v0.12.0

A new voice menu beside the model menu lets you pick how answers are read aloud: your device's own voice, or a more natural downloaded one. Choosing a voice downloads it, with a visible progress bar, and it works offline.

  • Two downloadable voices, Kokoro (97 MB) and Kitten (28 MB), both English and checked file by file against pinned hashes. Everyone can choose any of them on any device; the app only adds advice such as "recommended for this device" or "may be slow here".
  • If a download fails, the menu says why. Trying again asks you to confirm once and then goes ahead, as often as you like. Nothing is locked out.
  • The voice is used for Listen, voice calls and Roberta. The menu and Settings > Voice show what is on your device and have a Remove button.
  • The program that runs the voices contains eSpeak NG, so Private Local Chat is released under the GNU General Public License, version 3. The notices list every component and its source.

Related help: Privacy.

Two more on-device models, and a new layout

2 November 2026 · Release: the next published release after v0.12.0

Choose LFM2.5-2.6B (Liquid) or Granite 4.0 H 1B (IBM) from the model menu next to the message box, and remove any downloaded model from Settings.

  • LFM2.5-2.6B (about 1.37 GB, LFM Open License) and Granite 4.0 H 1B (about 0.90 GB, Apache-2.0) are served from the project's own storage, checked file by file against pinned hashes. Neither is picked for you: the app still starts with Qwen3.5.
  • Settings > Models lists every model with its size on this device, with Unload and Remove buttons, so downloads never pile up unseen. Storage & backups has the same Remove buttons.
  • The app has a new layout: a rail and sidebar, a model menu, a Settings page and a search that matches by words and meaning (Ctrl or Cmd+K).

Related help: Privacy.

Share, transfer settings, and help

1 November 2026 · Release: the next published release after v0.12.0

Three clearly separate ways to move things around, and a public help area that opens without setting up a model.

  • Share Private Local Chat: recommend the app with your device's share sheet, a copied link or a QR code. It always shares the public address https://privatelocal.chat/ and nothing from your chats or settings.
  • Transfer settings: review the endpoint, model and theme, then create a versioned link, QR code or settings file. An API token is included only if you choose, and it is encrypted with a password you pick. Tell the recipient the password separately.
  • Transfer chats and backups: exporting conversations and creating an encrypted backup are now named, reviewed steps, with the contents listed before the file is made. Provider settings travel separately through Transfer settings.
  • New Help, About, Privacy and Updates pages with a FAQ, troubleshooting steps and dated known issues. They are saved with the app, so they open offline once the app is saved on the device.
  • Voice conversation now works like a phone call: a green call button, a short ring while it connects, a call screen with a running timer, Mute, Skip and End call buttons, a "Call ended" summary with the length of the call, and optional ring, pick-up and hang-up sounds that you can turn off in Memory & preferences. On a phone the call takes the whole screen.
  • Voice notes: any reply can be played as a voice note (pause, jump to a sentence, change the speed, show the words), and you can record a long voice note of your own and listen back before sending. It uses your browser's own voices and speech recognition; the app never saves the audio.
  • Roberta, the app's robot, introduces the app before any model is loaded: a voicemail on phones, an incoming call on computers. Roberta is a program, not a person, with no feelings or awareness, and is not a language model. Press a number on the keypad, or say what you want, to hear about privacy, setup, sharing, backups, export or help.
  • What you say to Roberta is matched to prepared answers by meaning, on your device, with a small search model (about 37 MB, downloaded once from assets.privatelocal.chat and kept in the browser). It generates nothing and works while your model downloads. See Privacy for details.

Related help: Share, Transfer settings, Forgot the transfer password, Transfer chats, Privacy.

Known issues: iPhone page restarts during loading or long replies.

On-device chat without a graphics card

31 October 2026 · Release v0.12.0

When the browser has no usable GPU, the smaller Qwen3.5-2B model now runs on the processor (WebAssembly) instead of being unavailable.

  • It is slower than a graphics card, and the app says so while it sets up.
  • It is not offered on iPhone or iPad, on devices reporting under 4 GB of memory, or without WebAssembly SIMD.
  • The larger Qwen3.5-9B model still needs a GPU.

Related help: Will it work on my device?, A model will not load.

Known issues: On-device chat on the processor is slow, 2B model did not start on some Linux desktops without 16-bit shaders.

Voice conversation, steadier downloads

31 October 2026 · Release v0.11.0

Speak to the app and hear replies, and recover on their own from stalled downloads and old saved copies.

  • Voice conversation listens, sends after a pause and reads the reply aloud sentence by sentence.
  • Dictation clears when you send, so late words never land in the next message.
  • A download that makes no progress for two minutes is restarted by itself and reuses the files already saved.
  • An old saved copy of the app repairs itself when a newer version is published.

Related help: Where is voice processed?, Speech controls missing, A download stalls.

Known issues: Dictation and voice conversation missing in some in-app browsers.

The first message sets up on-device chat

31 October 2026 · Release v0.10.0

Send a message and the app picks a model for your device, downloads it, loads it and answers, with no separate setup step.

  • A progress card shows the stage, size and time left, and offers a smaller model on a desktop.
  • Stopping during setup removes the empty reply and keeps your question.

Related help: Getting started, Why does it download a model?.

Clearer messages when the graphics card is not usable

31 October 2026 · Release v0.9.1

The app now says what is actually wrong with WebGPU on a device, with things to try, instead of blaming one missing feature.

Related help: A model will not load.

New look, automatic model loading, browser speech

30 October 2026 · Release v0.9.0

The PrivateLocal.Chat brand arrived, a downloaded model loads by itself when the app opens, and read-aloud and dictation use your browser's speech services.

Related help: Where is voice processed?.

Everyday chat features, storage and backups

30 October 2026 · Release v0.8.0

Rendered answers with message controls, search, projects, documents, personal instructions and memory, plus readiness, storage, encrypted backups and an app lock.

  • Attach text, Markdown, CSV, code and text-based PDF files; they are read on your device.
  • Downloaded, loaded and ready-for-offline are shown as separate facts.
  • Back up chats, create an encrypted backup protected by a passphrase, or lock the app with a passphrase screen.
  • PDF reading was fixed on older Safari versions.

Related help: Where are my saved conversations?, What does App lock protect?, Restoring a backup.

No fixed reply length cap

30 October 2026 · Release v0.7.0

Replies are no longer cut off by a fixed length cap, and the text already written is kept when you stop a reply.

  • A reply can still end when the model's working window is full; the message says so and offers Continue.

Known issues: Very long replies end when the working window is full.

More reliable model downloads and offline use

30 October 2026 · Release v0.6.0

Interrupted downloads resume, verified files are kept, and the model loads when the download finishes.

  • Each file is retried on transient failures without losing files already verified.
  • An installed offline update can be activated with an explicit reload.
  • The Stop control stays available during generation.

Related help: A download stalls, Can I use it offline?.

Known issues: Model downloads fail when the phone is nearly full.

Hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with on-device models

29 October 2026 · Release v0.5.0

Private Local Chat moved to https://privatelocal.chat and gained on-device chat with Qwen3.5 models, alongside optional connected providers.

  • Model files are served from the project's own storage at assets.privatelocal.chat.
  • Feedback forms on GitHub were added in v0.4.0 earlier the same day.

Related help: What is Private Local Chat?, Privacy.
