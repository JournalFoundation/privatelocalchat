      About PrivateLocal.Chat
      
        
      
      
      
      
        
      Private AI chat on your device.
      
        
      PrivateLocal.Chat gives you a straightforward place to ask questions and work through ideas. Choose a model for your device or explicitly connect an outside provider.
      

        
      On this device
      
        
      In on-device mode, the model processes conversation content on this device.
      

        
      Connected provider
      
        
      A connected provider receives the messages and instructions included in your requests. Its data practices and any fees apply.
      

        
      Where your chats are kept
      
        
      Saved conversations are kept in browser storage. Export a backup of work you want to keep.
      

        
      Keep the app close at hand
      
        
      Use your browser’s install or Add to Home Screen option when available. Check offline readiness before relying on the app without a connection.
      

        
      Free to use on your device. Optional providers may charge. You decide when you are done.
